You probably owe sales tax in states you have never registered in.

The moment your sales crossed a threshold in another state, you got a filing obligation there. Most states set it around $100,000 in sales or 200 transactions in a year. If you sell on Shopify into 30 states, you have likely tripped several without noticing, because nothing in Shopify tells you when it happens.

The exposure compounds quietly. Uncollected tax becomes a liability you owe out of your own margin, plus penalties and interest, and it keeps growing every month you do not register. Brands usually find out the expensive way: a notice from a state, or a question in due diligence that stalls a fundraise or an acquisition.

The reason it stays unsolved is that the existing options are bad. Software makes you do the work. A bookkeeper is slow and works blind, with no live view of where your exposure is building. So it sits on a someday list until a state forces it to the top.

How Tassetta is different

You watch it. We file it.

Tassetta is a platform and a service in one. The platform gives you a live view of your entire sales tax position. The service is the part nobody else does for you: a tax expert who actually files.

You — the live view

You connect Shopify and your marketplace channels once. From there the platform runs on its own: it pulls your orders and refunds, tracks your economic nexus across all 50 states and DC against current thresholds, and computes every return automatically. You log in any time and see exactly where you have crossed, where you are getting close, where you are clear, and what each upcoming return looks like. Nothing is hidden behind us.

We — the accountable filing

The one step that stays human is the one that should. Before any return goes out, a Tassetta tax expert reviews it against the tax you actually collected and resolves anything that looks off. You approve in one click. Then we file, remit, register you in new states as you cross, and handle any notice a state sends back.

That is the difference in one line. Avalara and TaxJar sell you a calculator and a dashboard, and you still file. A bookkeeper files but gives you no live view. Tassetta gives you both: the platform shows you everything in real time, and a person who is accountable does the filing.

Marketplace-aware

We know the difference between your sales and Amazon’s.

If you sell on Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, or TikTok Shop, those marketplaces already collect and remit sales tax for you as the facilitator. The mistake brands and generic tools make is counting that tax as yours, which means you either over-remit or you cannot reconcile your own books.

Tassetta separates facilitator-collected sales from your direct Shopify sales automatically. You file on what is actually yours to file. Your liability matches your books, and you are not paying twice.

Compare

Tassetta vs sales tax software

Avalara and TaxJar are good at what they are: tax calculation engines and filing dashboards. The question is not whether they work. It is who does the work.

With software, you are the operator. You configure the product mappings, you watch the nexus dashboard, you reconcile the numbers, and you click to file, or you pay extra for managed filing on top of the software fee. The tool is one more thing your team logs into. And the billing usually scales with your transaction volume, so the cost climbs as you grow.

With Tassetta, we are the operator. A tax expert reviews every return. You approve in one click. You do not log into a calculation engine, you do not reconcile marketplace tax by hand, and you pay a flat fee per state instead of per transaction.

Tassetta vs a bookkeeper or CPA

A good CPA can absolutely handle your sales tax. The problem is usually how, not who. Most run on spreadsheets and manual logins to state portals. That means no live view of where your exposure is building, slower turnaround, and a bill that grows with the hours.

Tassetta does the same job on a platform built for it. Your nexus is tracked in real time, not recalculated at quarter-end. Returns are reconciled against your actual collected tax automatically. Where a CPA might be the better call: if your tax situation is entangled with complex income tax, entity structure, or international questions, you want a full accounting relationship, and sales tax is just one piece.

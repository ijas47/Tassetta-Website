Who Tassetta is for

Tassetta is built for ecommerce brands that have outgrown “we only collect in our home state” but do not want to turn sales tax into a job for someone on the team.

You are a fit if

You sell on Shopify, or Shopify plus marketplaces. You ship to customers in many states. You are doing roughly $1M to $20M in sales. And you have a quiet suspicion that you have crossed tax thresholds in places you have not dealt with yet.

That suspicion is almost always right. That is the exact problem we exist to close.

You are probably not a fit yet if

You only sell in one state, or you are pre-revenue. You do not have a sales tax problem yet, and we would rather tell you that than sell you something you do not need. Come back when you start shipping across state lines at volume.

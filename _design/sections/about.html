About Tassetta

Tassetta exists because the two ways to handle sales tax both put the work back on the brand. Software hands you a dashboard and a calculation API and calls it solved. A bookkeeper does it by hand, slowly, with no real-time view of your risk. Neither one actually takes it off your plate.

So we built the thing in between: a tax team that does the work, running on a platform we built ourselves so the work is faster, more accurate, and fully documented. You get the outcome of hiring an expert with the speed and transparency of good software, without operating either one.

What we believe about the work

1

A return that goes out under your name should be reviewed by a person who is accountable for it.

2

Your compliance cost should be predictable.

3

The day an auditor asks for your history, the proof should already exist.

Those three beliefs are why the product works the way it does.
